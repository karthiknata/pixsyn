Website Design

Most business websites are built once and forgotten — a digital brochure nobody updates. We build the opposite: a site engineered around one goal, getting the right visitor to reach out, with a launch timeline that actually holds.

Every project starts with your offer and your ideal client, not a template. We map the pages your business actually needs, write the structure around how people search and decide, and build in Webflow or WordPress depending on what fits your business best — not what's easiest for us.

The 14-day launch guarantee isn't a marketing line. It's the direct result of a tight, senior-led process with no junior handoffs and no waiting on a queue.

Common Questions
Is the 14-day launch guarantee real, or does it only apply to simple sites?+

It applies to standard business websites — the kind most Saskatchewan service businesses need (5-8 pages, clear service offering). Larger custom builds with heavy functionality get a specific timeline on the strategy call, but the standard build is genuinely 14 days from kickoff.

Webflow or WordPress — how do you decide which one I need?+

It depends on how much you'll want to edit yourself after launch and what you might need to integrate later. We'll walk through both on the strategy call and recommend the one that fits your business, not just whichever is faster for us to build.

What if I need more than 5 pages?+

Totally fine — pricing and timeline scale with scope, and we'll lay that out clearly before any work starts. No surprise invoices.

Do you write the copy, or do I need to provide it?+

We handle messaging and structure as part of the build — you're not left staring at a blank page trying to write your own homepage copy. If you already have brand copy you love, we'll work with it.

What's Included
  • Custom website design and build
  • Webflow or WordPress delivery
  • 14-day launch guarantee
  • Conversion-focused page structure and CTAs
  • Mobile-first responsive design
  • Analytics setup and SEO foundations

Local SEO

Ranking locally isn't about tricking Google — it's about giving it clear, consistent signals that your business is real, active, and actually the right answer for what someone nearby is searching for.

Our Local SEO retainer covers the fundamentals most agencies skip: Google Business Profile optimization, on-page structure that targets your actual service area, and ongoing content that keeps your site looking active instead of static. It's a monthly process, not a one-time fix, because rankings are earned over time, not purchased.

Common Questions
How long until I actually see ranking movement?+

Most businesses see early movement in Google Maps rankings within 6-8 weeks, with more meaningful organic search gains building over 3-6 months. Anyone promising overnight results isn't being straight with you.

Is this a long-term contract or month-to-month?+

We'll go over the specifics on your strategy call, but SEO is inherently a compounding process — leaving after two months rarely gives it enough time to work. We're upfront about that from the start.

Do you handle my Google Business Profile too?+

Yes — Maps visibility is often the single biggest lever for local service businesses, so profile optimization is a core part of the retainer, not an add-on.

What actually shows up in the monthly reporting?+

Ranking movement on your target keywords, traffic trends, what content or changes went live that month, and what we're prioritizing next — no vague "engagement" metrics that don't tie back to leads.

What's Included
  • Google Business Profile optimization
  • On-page SEO across your site
  • Local content strategy
  • Monthly reporting on rankings and traffic
  • Ongoing technical SEO maintenance

AI Receptionist

Every missed call is a customer who might not call back. An AI receptionist answers instantly, asks the right questions, qualifies the lead, and books the appointment — even when you're on a job, closed for the day, or simply can't get to the phone in time.

It's trained specifically on your business: your services, your pricing logic, your service area, and your booking process. It's not a generic phone tree. Urgent calls can be routed straight to you, while everything else gets handled without interrupting your day.

Common Questions
Will customers know they're talking to AI?+

Sometimes they'll notice, sometimes they won't — what matters more is that they get a real, helpful answer instead of a voicemail. We're not trying to trick anyone; we're trying to make sure nobody hits silence.

What happens with emergencies or urgent calls?+

The AI is trained to recognize urgency and route those calls directly to you or your team immediately, instead of treating every call the same way.

Does it work with my existing business phone number?+

Yes — we set it up to work with your current number, so there's nothing new for customers to learn or remember.

How does it actually learn my business?+

During setup we go through your services, pricing structure, service area, hours, and common customer questions, and train the system specifically around that — not a generic script.

What's Included
  • 24/7 call answering and lead capture
  • Custom training on your services and pricing
  • Intelligent call routing for urgent requests
  • CRM and scheduling integration
  • Conversation summaries after every call

AI Website Chatbot

Most visitors who leave your website without contacting you had a question they didn't want to call and ask. An embedded AI chatbot answers those questions instantly — pricing logic, service details, availability — and turns a browsing visitor into a warm, qualified lead your team actually wants to talk to.

It's trained specifically on your offer, not a generic FAQ script, and hands off cleanly to your team once a lead is ready — no cold, vague enquiries landing in your inbox.

Common Questions
How is this different from a generic chatbot plugin?+

Plug-and-play chatbots are trained on nothing — they either give vague non-answers or just collect an email. Ours is trained specifically on your services, your pricing structure, and your actual FAQs, so it can give real answers.

Can it actually answer pricing questions?+

Yes, within the logic we set up together — general ranges, what affects pricing, and when it should hand off to a real quote instead of guessing.

Where do the leads it captures actually go?+

Straight into your CRM or inbox, formatted with the context of the conversation — not just a bare name and email with no context attached.

What's Included
  • Embedded chat widget on your website
  • Trained on your offer, pricing logic, and FAQs
  • Lead qualification built into the conversation
  • Clean handoff to your team or CRM
  • Always-on, 24/7 coverage
