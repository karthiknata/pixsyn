When a DIY Website Builder Is Actually the Right Choice

Sometimes Wix is exactly the right choice. Sometimes it's the expensive choice.

Most agencies will tell you never to use Wix. They're wrong. Sometimes it's exactly what a business needs. The problem is knowing when you've outgrown it.

DIY Makes Sense When…

You're just starting. You need a simple brochure website. You have a tiny budget. You don't depend on online leads. You simply need somewhere people can find your phone number. That's perfectly reasonable.

Where DIY Starts Falling Apart

Eventually your business grows. Now you need better SEO, faster performance, CRM integrations, AI chat, booking systems, automation, and custom pages. That's where templates become limitations.

The Hidden Cost

The monthly subscription isn't expensive. Your time is. Every hour spent fixing layouts, plugins, SEO, forms, or mobile issues is an hour you're not serving customers. That's the real cost.

A Website Isn’t the Product

This is where many businesses get stuck. They think buying a website means buying growth. It doesn't. Growth comes from the system behind it — traffic, conversions, automation, follow-up. That's what actually produces customers.

The right website isn't the cheapest one. It's the one that stops costing you time every week.
