Five Signs Your Website Isn't the Problem

Sometimes your website is doing its job. Your business systems aren't.

A surprising number of businesses ask us for a new website. After one conversation, we tell them to keep the one they already have.

Because the problem isn't attracting customers. It's what happens after someone becomes a lead. Here are the signs.

1. You Forget to Follow Up

If leads sit in your inbox for two days, the website isn't broken. Your workflow is. Automation can respond within seconds.

2. Information Lives Everywhere

Phone notes. Sticky notes. Text messages. Emails. Your CRM. Nothing talks to anything else. That's costing time every day.

3. You’re Entering the Same Information Twice

Customer calls. You write it down. Then type it into software later. Then send another email. Those steps should happen automatically.

4. You’re Answering the Same Questions Every Day

Pricing. Hours. Service areas. Availability. Those answers shouldn't depend on someone picking up the phone.

5. Growth Feels Chaotic

More customers should mean more revenue. Not more stress. If hiring another person feels like the only solution, automation is probably missing somewhere.

Automation Doesn’t Replace People

It removes repetitive work. Your team spends more time helping customers instead of copying information between systems. That's the goal.

The best businesses aren't always the busiest. They're usually the ones with the best systems working behind the scenes.
